COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Keep it constructive.

  • Respect other players, modders, and contributors.
  • Keep feedback focused on the project, gameplay mechanics, and story content.
  • Keep major Book II and Book III plot twists inside spoiler-tagged channels so new players can experience the story fresh.
