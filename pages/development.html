Engineering & Worldbuilding Notes

MILESTONE 01 · AUGUST 2026

The Spark: How a Crazy Idea Became a Geopolitical Fantasy Epic

By KCoder-programming · Official Faltu Studios & KCoder Productions

It started as a late-night joke between friends at Official Faltu Studios. We were debating: what if ancient mythological dynasties like Mahishmati didn't just fight with medieval swords, but had supersonic attack helicopters, encrypted satellite war rooms, and Russian intercontinental ballistic missiles?

From that premise, the character of Momo emerged—not a traditional hero, but a chillingly brilliant strategist whose cold calculation makes him both savior and eventual tyrant. We drafted the first three-act narrative arc: Revenge Ready, Reveal Beddy, and Business Shaddy.

MILESTONE 02 · SEPTEMBER 2026 Story spoilers · click to read

Designing Momo, Mota Bhai, and Keerpada: Dark Occult Meets Military Tech

By KCoder-programming · KCoder Productions

The biggest creative challenge was ensuring the occult elements felt visceral and consequential. When Mota Bhai's wife is brutally slain by Keerpada, it permanently breaks Mota Bhai—turning him from a jovial tea-drinker into a hollow vessel of dark arts.

For the climactic North Pole battle, we grounded Momo's divine victory in Einstein's energy-mass equivalence: E = mc². If Keerpada is immortal non-physical energy, Momo uses cosmic gravitational pressure to condense him into solid matter: the Notorius Metal.

MILESTONE 03 · OCTOBER 2026 Story spoilers · click to read

The Underdog Rebellion: Writing Thumza and Unmasking the Kalaka Truth

By KCoder-programming · Official Faltu Studios & KCoder Productions

In Book III, the player realizes the truth about the Kalaka people: they weren't savage beasts. Mahishmati poisoned their ancestral rivers with uranium tailings to fund their golden palaces.

Thumza Curry Madari represents the opposite of Momo. Momo fought for his ultimate ego ("a mere sacrifice for the ultimate me"), while Thumza fought with zero desire for the throne ("I didn't come here for your chair, Momo. I came here to burn it"). The epilogue 'The Broken Wheel' is the soul of our game.
