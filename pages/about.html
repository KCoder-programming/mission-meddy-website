THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PROJECT

ABOUT &
CREDITS

Mission Meddy is a free, source-available independent game project built around an original dark geopolitical action-fantasy universe and a complete three-part story.

OFFICIAL PROJECT IDENTITY

One project, united vision.

Mission Meddy is created and produced under the collaborative banner of KCoder Productions and Official Faltu Studios.

ORIGINAL CREATOR & DEVELOPER

KCoder-programming

Original creator, lead software engineer, and game producer. All official source code repositories and software builds on GitHub are published under the KCoder-programming identity.

PRODUCTION BANNER

KCoder Productions

Creative production label founded by KCoder-programming overseeing the engineering, narrative adaptation, and multi-platform distribution (Web, Android, PC) of the Mission Meddy trilogy.

PARTNER GAME STUDIO

Official Faltu Studios

Partner creative game studio co-founding the Mission Meddy franchise, worldbuilding, and satirical dark geopolitical universe alongside KCoder Productions.
