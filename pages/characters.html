PEOPLE OF THE SAGA

CHARACTERS

Rulers, strategists, rebels and people caught between them. These spoiler-free profiles introduce the key figures from the trilogy.

DHOLAPUR / MAHISHMATI
THE CENTRAL FIGURE

Momo

An exceptionally capable strategist exiled from Mahishmati who saved Dholapur from an ancient menace. When a sudden invasion shatters his estate, his journey sets off a chain reaction across empires.


SPECIALTY Geopolitical Strategy & Tactical Warfare
LYARI INTELLIGENCE NETWORK
THE OPERATIVE

Thumza Curry Madari

A rugged, battle-hardened Lyari field operative who dives headfirst into fortified offshore strongholds. Driven by an unyielding moral code in a world ruled by ruthless monarchs.

SPECIALTY Solo Infiltration & Undercover Black-Ops
MAHISHMATI
THE OCCULTIST

Mota Bhai

Momo's oldest friend in Mahishmati. Behind his warm, tea-loving demeanor lies a terrifying mastery of ancient dark invocations and a phonebook containing the private numbers of the world's deadliest rulers.

SPECIALTY Forbidden Ancient Rituals & Contacts
UNDERWORLD OF LYARI
THE SPYMASTER

Jameen Udadi

A sharp-witted, paternal intelligence chief originally from Dholapur who commands Lyari's underground bunker network. For Jameen, his field operatives are family, not disposable pawns.


SPECIALTY Global Covert Intelligence & Comms
KALAKA FORCES
THE HIDDEN HAND

Meddy

Leader of the mysterious Kalaka forces that march from the borderlands. Driven by a long history of loss and vengeance against the golden walls of Mahishmati.

SPECIALTY Guerrilla Command & Ambush Tactics
SUPERPOWER OF AMRICAPURI
THE DICTATOR

Thumpasur (Thumpa)

Momo's elder cousin and the terrifying dictator of the newly risen industrial superpower of Amricapuri. Cloaked in heavy tactical armor and fur, he rules through brute military force.

SPECIALTY Armored Tank Divisions & Revolver
KINGDOM OF MAHISHMATI
THE MATRIARCH

Rajmata Melody

The iron-handed ruler of the golden juggernaut of Mahishmati. Guardian of the royal bloodline and keeper of ancient court secrets that shape the fate of every nation.

SPECIALTY Imperial Statecraft & Dynastic Power
AMRICAPURI SECRET SERVICE (A.S.S.)
THE ALLY

Pappu Pandey

An unpredictable deep-cover mole working right inside the belly of the Amricapuri Secret Service. Though eccentric on the surface, his intercepted manifests repeatedly pull off miracles.


SPECIALTY Deep-Cover Mole & Dual Silenced SMGs
FREEZING EMPIRE OF RUSSIA
THE RUSSIAN LEADER

Vladiveer Protien

The unstoppable leader of the hyper-militarized northern empire of Russia. A close ally to Mahishmati whose elite divisions and high-tech weaponry command global dread.

SPECIALTY Supersonic ICBMs & Arctic Divisions
